## 0. What G2 is, and where it sits

G2 runs the virtual MEA (ANN's `process_campaign.py`) again over every raw hhgap simulation, the Giulia
project's simulated cultures, with the Giulia device's geometry, so that the simulated arm comes out of the
same pipeline as the real one (G1, done 2026-10-02) and says so in a record. It is C8 for the other project:
the same plan, array and gate, with another campaign set, another geometry, another output root and its own
record. Plain version: detect spikes once more on every Giulia simulation, with one virtual electrode placed
and sized like the real device's, and keep a receipt.

Inputs: `ANN/Phenomenological/Main/Giulia_Astro/campaign_cadex_hhgap_v*` (v1, v2, v4, v5 in plan v1.7) and the
Giulia cohort manifest `Giulia_Astro/extracted_giulia/cohort_manifest.json` (sha256 `21b7ef03bb7f776b`; 16
wells, `electrodes_per_subset` 1, `fs_raw` 10000.0). Output: `ANN/MEA_analysis/mea_out_giulia_v2/` with
`REEXTRACTION_RECORD.json` (+ `.sha256`). Consumers: G3 (the label freeze over the kept tasks), B5/G4 (the
sim-window bank). Waits for: the C8 record (D-057), because both runs share `ANN/MEA_analysis/` and the C8 gate
re-checks it (sec. 2).
