## Verdict
PASS WITH ISSUES. The synthetic experiment was run end to end through its
real entry point, `run_pipeline.sh` (the sequencer as a chain of PBS jobs
under the fake PBS, D-098). The run used a reduced copy of the committed
`synth.json` v4: arm S of 4 shards, R0 and R3 of 24 windows each, and four
searches. These were S-A1 on R0, S-A2-only on R0, S-A5-only on R3, and a
mixed S-A2 search on R3 with `alt_best_fit` true for G_q. The chain ends
`PIPELINE DONE` in about 4 minutes, with 12 stages done in sec. 2.1's order
and no stage job submitted twice. Every quantity recomputed here without the
code under test agrees with what the chain wrote: the banks (rows, W 128,
classes, wells, ids, seeds, builds, record digests), the split (34/4/2
donors, one hash from the tool and from every runner), the trials' flags
and the steps per epoch, L_pseudo_real over the 24 cohort windows, the
finalists ranked on nll alone, the ensembles' L_bar (scipy's logsumexp, to
1e-12), and the other arm's finalist. The following also hold: two runs
with the same seeds are bit-identical; a run stopped at the bank boundary,
with its driver deleted mid-search and stopped again mid best fit, resumes
to the same numbers with every job once. The refusals tried here stop the
chain where the spec says: a foreign bank folder, a search folder of
another identity, a configuration sec. 3.1 refuses, a search finalist that
is the best of neither arm, and a search artefact gone. The record tool's
refusals and a search with no ok trial are tier 1's.

Five findings, none a wrong number on the committed path:
- The earliest search does not get the free cores first (F501): 20 to 24
  inversions per run, the S-A1 round waiting 79 s while 11 jobs of later
  searches started.
- The configuration's `search.flow.num_bins` never reaches the runner
  (F502): the identity says 8 while every flow has 10 bins.
- An identity key changed with `--accept-config-change` while a search runs
  is not refused (F503): one search ranked trials of two report splits.
- The best fit reuses member records of another finalist (F504).
- A shard lost after its bank's record carries down to a search on the
  wrong bank, with no line in the log (F505, t1-B's F205 seen along the
  chain).

Six problems in the spec (S501-S506).

configuration, the fake PBS's environment, the entry point, readers of the
spool and of the outputs; it computes no expected value) and `conftest.py`
(the runs, kept under `$C2_ROOT` and reused when finished). Files are named
`test_spec_chain_C2_<topic>.py` (C2 without "t2-": a hyphen does not import).
Each chain run is the reduced project under the fake PBS through
`run_pipeline.sh --config <file>`. The script is the same as `--project
synth`, but the file lives outside `projects/`, where the tester may not
write. One run is about 4 min.

## Not tested, and why
- davinci (sec. 7): the real PBS, conda, the full-size banks (20 shards of
  2,000 wells, W 18,000), the 60-trial searches and 5-member ensembles.
  Under the fake PBS, `qdel` kills a running driver at once, whereas davinci
  sends SIGTERM and then SIGKILL; only the kill path was exercised (the
  successor continued).
- G_q on the committed v4: every `alt_best_fit` is false there, so G_q ran
  on a mixed S-A2 search (v3's shape), as T18 does. S-A25 was not run.
- The real bank as part of a search's identity (S503) was read from the code
  (`_identity`'s keys), not exercised with a changed `real`.
- NOT_RUNNABLE: the Sbi-extractor commit 4ef7576 (D-113) is still on no
  branch of the public repository (`feat/real-arm-parity` at e9aeb557).
  None of the chain's suites needs it; block 1c's launcher check passed on
  e9aeb55.
- Outside this chain, noticed only:
  - `run_pipeline.sh --project synth --dry-run` on the committed file is
    t1-A's.
  - The evaluation reading `BEST_FIT_COMPLETE.json` (blocks 19-23) is
    t2-C3's.
  - The purge of a synthetic project (block 24) is t1-D's.
  - Blocks 25-26 (`label_axes`, the term-only campaigns) shape v4 and were

## Problems in the spec
- S501: D-099's two statements disagree about the successor driver.
  - Sec. 4 says the cores of jobs "queued, held or running, the sequencer's
    own job included" never exceed `resources.max_cores`.
  - Block 1 (d) budgets one sequencer job (`budget = max_cores -
    driver.ncpus`), while the D-098 chain always holds a successor driver
    job waiting on `afterany`.
  - Run 1's fake-PBS records peak at 3 cores without that successor and 4
    with it (cap 3). The spec should say whether a job waiting on its
    dependency counts.
- S502: done(F_q) and done(G_q) (sec. 2.1) are the artefact's existence.
  Sec. 2.2 therefore keeps a best fit done after its search was run again
  with another finalist. With `--retry best_fit_S-A1`, the stage was
  `skipped`, and BEST_FIT_COMPLETE.json's finalist 2154c97a96f5 stood
  against SEARCH_COMPLETE.json's b00214a690e9. The spec does not say
  whether done(F_q) should check the finalist (and see F504 for the
  members).
- S503: The identity (block 7) covers the simulated bank only. On the
  synthetic kind every trial is also given q.real's bank (D-122: scored on
  it; A2 and A5 train on it), yet neither the identity, the trial id nor
  `pipeline_rounds.json` records which real bank was used. A changed
  `synthetic.searches[i].real`, or a rebuilt real bank, under an existing
  search folder is therefore not refused [reasoning from the code read:
  `_identity` and `_rounds_file`'s keys; not executed].
- S504: Sec. 2.1 states the priority as a consequence ("so the free cores
  go to the earliest search ... first") without the rule that would produce
  it. Block 1 (d)'s Submitter and the stages' polls give none, and F501
  shows it does not follow.
- S505: Sec. 6 lists only `--dry-run` for synth. The chain's real entry
  point (`run_pipeline.sh --project synth`, and its `--status` and `--stop`)
  is listed for giulia only.
- S506: Sec. 3.1 does not require `T_win * fs` to be an integer. With W =
  round(T_win f_s) (sec. 2.4; the builder and the record alike), T_win
  12.85 s at 10 Hz makes 128-sample windows of 12.8 s without a word.
- Tier 1 already lists that secs. 1.1, 2.4, 2.5 and block 17 still
  describe v1-v3 (7 label axes, scale 0.6, mixed searches with alt fits)
  while the chain runs v4 (t1-B's S201-S208, t1-D's S701-S712). The
  committed configuration of this chain rests on blocks 25 and 26, both
  "Confirmed: no".
