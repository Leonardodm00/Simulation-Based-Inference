## Problems in the spec

- S1. Block 35's fix-round note ("the other rows' densities equal
  (difference 0)") and R35f's "logq_ref_members on the other rows" are
  reproduced without a tolerance. R36h's "the NLL of every other row equals
  the unmodified run's" has the same gap. Exact equality is not a property
  of the code. When a row leaves the box, the in-box rows' batches change
  composition, and the float32 flow gives other roundoff. With the boundary
  rows chosen here (the second draw row and the third non-draw row) the
  non-draw rows differ by up to 3.8e-6 nats (S-A1:A1) and 1.3e-5 nats
  (S-A2-R0:A2), while the draw rows are bit for bit. The smoke checks use
  1e-4 (R35b's tolerance), and the entries should say so.
- S2. Sec. 2.6 defines "outside the open box" as a coordinate <= 0 or >= 1.
  A NaN coordinate is neither, and the spec does not say what the
  evaluation does with it. The bank reader rejects it (F2).
- S3. Block 36's "an ensemble whose sample is absent ... is left out" does
  not say whether a provenance whose listed files are missing counts as
  absent. Block 38's "done" says it does not count as current (F3).
- S4 (mathematical). Sec. 2.6 keeps the rows outside the open box in "SBC
  and TARP over the calibration rows". For a calibration row with
  theta_{i,k} = 1 exactly, the marginal rank #{draws < theta} is n_post
  whatever the posterior, and with theta_{i,k} = 0 it is 0. Such a row is
  not a draw from the prior on the open box, so its rank is fixed by the
  label and not by calibration. With a handful of such rows among
  thousands, SBC's KS test is barely moved. If a recorded bank has many
  labels clipped to a bound, SBC on that axis would reject for a reason
  unrelated to the posterior's calibration, and TARP's coverage would shift
  the same way. Observed here: the boundary row's ranks on its edited axis
  equal #{draws < 1.0}, checked against the definition. Whether such rows
  should leave SBC and TARP as well, or be counted in their output, is the
  project's call.
