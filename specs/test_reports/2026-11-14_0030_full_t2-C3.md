C3-O1 The stages, their order and dependencies (block 22 Rule; block 23)
- O1a The units are `calbank:<k>` for k = 0 .. cal_shards-1 and one
  `sample:<q>:<arm>` per complete ensemble of `ev_plan.ensembles`. There is
  one `report`. `data` is a unit only when `--what` names it (the default
  is `calbank,sample,report`).
- O1b Every sampling job depends `afterok` on every calibration job
  submitted now or live. The report job depends `afterok` on every sampling
  job submitted now or live. The data job depends on nothing.
- O1c The report is not submitted while no ensemble is sampled or being
  sampled (`report: nothing to report yet`).
- O1d One job = `eval_run.pbs` in `envs.train`. Its command is one argument
  per line in `<out_root>/jobs/<unit>.argv`, and its log goes under
  `<out_root>/jobs/logs/`. Job names are at most 15 characters (`ev_cal<k>`,
  `ev_s<i><b|a>`, `ev_report`, `ev_data`). `ROADMAP_STAGE` is I19 (calbank,
  samplings), I22 (report) or I23 (data). The last log line is
  `EVAL JOB EXIT: <n>`.
- O1e Nothing of the pipeline's state is read or written: `<work_root>`'s
  files are byte-identical before and after the chain.
- O1f Exit codes: 0 with `EVAL SUBMIT OK: <n> job(s) submitted, <d> done,
  <l> live` (or `EVAL DRY RUN: ... nothing written`); 2 on the arguments or
  the configurations; 3 on a refusal, with nothing submitted; 4 when a qsub
  fails.

C3-O2 The done(s) predicates and the digests that hand an artefact on
- O2a calbank:k is done iff shard k and its sidecar exist with the build's
  arguments (`ev_plan.calbank_build`). A sidecar of other arguments, an
  unreadable one, or a shard index beyond the build is a refusal; nothing is
  overwritten.
- O2b sample:q:arm is done iff `<stem>_sample.json` lists files that all
  exist, with the current `ev_config.SAMPLE_PARAMS` and the current sha256
  of its best fit's `BEST_FIT_COMPLETE.json`. Otherwise it is stale or
  absent, and the reason is printed. `oracle_tol` is not a SAMPLE_PARAM.
- O2c The report is submitted iff a sampling job is submitted now or live,
  or `REPORT_COMPLETE.json` records inputs other than
  `ev_plan.report_inputs` (the samples' provenance digests and
  REPORT_PARAMS). A live report is not submitted twice.
- O2d data is done iff `DATA_VIEW.json` records the current
  `ev_plan.data_inputs`: DATA_PARAMS and, per bank, each shard read with the
  sha256 of its sidecar.
- O2e `REPORT_COMPLETE.json` records the current `report_inputs` and as many
  figures as `FIGURES.md` lists.

C3-O3 The resume rule along the chain
- O3a A second submission after completion submits nothing.
- O3b A live job (queued, held, running) is not submitted twice.
- O3c A changed SAMPLE_PARAM makes every sample stale, and a changed
  `BEST_FIT_COMPLETE.json` makes its ensemble's sample stale. A changed
  report value makes the report alone stale. `--resample` re-runs a
  sampling and puts the report after it.
- O3d A chain interrupted at a stage boundary resumes with only the missing
  units.

C3-O4 The refusals that must stop the chain
- O4a ev_sample refuses when a rebuilt member differs from its per-row file
  (`nll` on D_sim, `nll_pseudo_real` on its own cohort) by more than
  `oracle_tol`: exit 3, naming the member, the largest and the mean
  difference and the tolerance, and writing nothing. It also refuses a
  record of another split and a folder of two arms.
- O4b The calibration bank is checked before any sampling (box prior, a
  seed no bank has, cal_n windows of as many donors); a refusal writes
  nothing.
- O4c ev_metrics leaves out an ensemble that is not sampled or was sampled
  with other values, and exits 3 when none is current.
- O4d ev_figures exits 3 without metrics.
- O4e ev_dataview exits 3 and writes nothing when there are fewer shards
  than sim_shards, a zoom is longer than a window, or a statistic is
  constant.
- O4f A sampling that refuses leaves the report queued after it (afterok)
  unrun: the chain stops before `REPORT_COMPLETE.json`.
- O4g An ensemble whose best fit is not complete is named `not complete: <q>
  (<folder>: ...)` and not sampled.

C3-O5 Quantities that must carry through unchanged
- O5a D_sim is the report rows of the members' grouped split
  (`split.fractions`, split seed `best_fit.split_seed`), in global row
  order. The split hash must equal every member record's. The rows match the
  members' `_perrow.npz` row for row (theta, donor). There is one split for
  every member, arm and search. The split takes whole donors:
  n_report = n_d - round(f_tr n_d) - round(f_sel n_d) (sec. 2.3).
- O5b (E1) The ensemble's mean NLL over D_sim equals block 7's `L_bar`, and
  each member's mean NLL equals its `L`. The tolerance is `oracle_tol`:
  block 19 bounds every member row for row by it, and logsumexp is
  1-Lipschitz in the max norm.
- O5c Each member's mean NLL on its search's own cohort equals its record's
  `L_pseudo_real` (sec. 2.4), within `oracle_tol`.
- O5d `logq_ref` = logsumexp_m(`logq_ref_members`) - log M, and it is -inf
  exactly where `in_box` = all(0 < theta^ref < 1) is false.
- O5e D_<r> is every window of the cohort's bank in bank order, with the
  bank's theta, donor, well and cls. n_R = n_classes x donors_per_class x
  wells x n_windows.
- O5f theta-dagger = theta* + 0.35 pi on the bank's free axes (block 25:
  `latent_spec.free_idx`), with the duty capped only when it is free. Block
  19's `<stem>_<r>dag.npz` and block 23's `data_view.npz` hold the same
  values.
- O5g The eight fixed statistics of a window are the same in blocks 19
  and 23.
- O5h Seeds: `seed_of(seed, D) + k` per member, common to the ensembles.
  Two runs with the same values give identical arrays.
- O5i Units: theta in the open unit box, NLLs in nats per row, the PPC
  windows on the bank's own scale.
- O5j Index bases: `rows` are int64 bank rows, and `ppc_rows` index into
  the data set. `draw_member` is n_post / M draws per member in member
  order. `ppc_draw_idx` = j n_post / ppc_draws. Donor ids are bank-wide
  (k * 10^6 + local).
- O5k The calibration bank has the simulated bank's W, f_s, T_win and
  n_neurons, one window per donor, the box prior, and a seed distinct from
  every bank seed.

C3-O6 Search-level carry-through (blocks 7, 18 -> 20)
- O6a `search_<q>.json`: the trials in `pipeline_rounds.json`'s order, each
  with the ledger's arm, status, nll and L_pseudo_real; the running minimum
  per arm over the ok trials; the finalist equal to
  `SEARCH_COMPLETE.json`'s; a term-only search's trials all of its arm.
- O6b Every member's learning curve equals its runner record's `history`.

C3-O7 Cross-chain items for steps 8-9

- O7c An artefact reused after its upstream changed: a bank rebuilt after

## Not tested, and why

- davinci (SPEC sec. 7): the real PBS (its `afterok` handling of a failed
  dependency, which the fake ends with exit -1, its log stage-out), conda,
  the full-size banks and ensembles (W 18,000, n_post 1,000, M 5), and the
  timings.
- The conda path of `eval_run.pbs` (`SKIP_CONDA=0`): the whole chain ran
  with `SKIP_CONDA=1`, as the existing tests do. On davinci the activation
  records `<WORK_ROOT>/envs/<env>.prefix` (D-111), so an evaluation job can
  write that file under the pipeline's work root. That was not exercised
  here, and C3-O1e was checked under `SKIP_CONDA=1` only.
- The roadmap's markers along the chain: `ROADMAP_DISABLE=1` (the jobs
  run from this checkout, whose origin is GitHub). Each job's
  `ROADMAP_STAGE` in its `-v` was checked. t1-C tested the markers by hand
  on a stand-in origin (its F303).
- `--project <tag>`: the chain used `--config/--eval-config`, since a
  project folder would have to be written into the checkout. The
  regression suite's E22a covers `--project` (passed).
- The committed `synth.json` / `synth_eval.json` themselves: only their
  dry runs ran (E22a, with `sbi_dir` at this checkout). The reduced project
  keeps every value of the committed v4 that the chain's logic reads
  (label axes, centre scale, split, the campaigns' shape) and cuts the
  sizes.
- Sbi-extractor (MINIMAL_RUN's SBIX_CLONE, NOT_RUNNABLE's note on
  4ef7576): `feat/real-arm-parity` is still at e9aeb55744 (4ef7576 absent).
  No suite or test of blocks 7 and 16-23 needs it (the suites that read
  SBIX_CLONE are blocks 1c, 2, 3, 8 and 9: sibling t1-A), so it was not
  cloned, and no C3 test is prevented.
- Block-internal oracles of blocks 7, 16-23: tier 1's (t1-A, t1-B, t1-C),
  cited, not re-derived. That includes L-C2ST's statistical behaviour, SBC,
