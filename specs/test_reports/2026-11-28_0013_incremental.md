## Verdict

PASS WITH ISSUES. Every block in scope runs through its real entry point on the minimal case. The tool's whole chain passed end to end, twice with the same seed, with identical numbers: `export`, `bundle-check --rebuild`, `card-template`, `card-check`, `windows`, `infer` (from a windows file and from a recording) and `device-figure`. The bundle's networks reproduce the pipeline's bit for bit: the state tensors, log q, the draws under a seed and the embeddings. An inference on the recording bank's windows, with block 35's seed, reproduces block 35's sample files and block 36's metrics: bit for bit for the draws, the densities, the embeddings and the six summaries, and within 1e-12 for the gate's arrays. The two fixes in blocks 35 and 36 do what the amended Rule says, and the previous report's two NOTEs are closed by them. Every regression suite gives the same count as in the previous report.

Two MAJOR findings:

- **F1 (blocks 44 and 46).** The trace the tool cuts into windows is the smoothed spike count per bin per electrode. Sec. 2.7 states counts "divided by n_e Delta t [Hz per electrode]", and the window figure labels its axis that way, so both are 1 / Delta t = 100 times off from the values drawn. The values themselves are the extractor's (its eq. (3) has no Delta t). They are therefore the same as the training banks' values, and no inference is affected. The spec, block 44's oracle T44a and block 46's axis label are what disagree with the code.
- **F2 (block 41).** Nothing protects the manifest itself. `bundle-check` passes a bundle in which the gate's `alpha`, a parameter's name or `card_complete` was changed. T41c requires exit 3 for one byte changed in the manifest.

Also one MINOR (F3: a bin, and with it a window, is lost when T_rec / Delta t is not exactly representable) and four NOTEs (F4-F7).

## Problems in the spec

- **S1 (the unit of a window).** Sec. 2.7 ("divided by n_e Delta t [Hz per electrode]"), block 44's T44a oracle, block 44's `traces` line and block 46's axis label all say Hz per electrode. The cited eq. (3) of EXTRACTOR_USAGE.md, and the code, give spikes per bin per electrode. The Notation row for x already records this for the bench banks (2026-11-12) and leaves the extractor's banks "not checked". They are checked now: the same unit (F1).
- **S2.** "`files`: a dict from every other member's name to its sha256" can be read as every member but the manifest, or (as built) the three fixed members other than the manifest, the members' safetensors being digested in `members[]`.
- **S3.** Sec. 0's line for the tool still says "Drafted, not built", while blocks 40-46 are smoke-tested. "Last reviewed at commit: 9fcbc88" is stale.
- **S4.** T41c asks that one byte changed in the manifest make `bundle-check` exit 3, but sec. 2.7 defines no digest of the manifest, so no mechanism is specified (F2). Sec. 2.7 also does not say whether `card_complete` must agree with the card, or the manifest's `gate` with `evaluation/sim.json`'s.
- **S5.** Sec. 2.7 defines K as "the number of bins of width Delta t in T_rec" and the bins as half-open, and also says that the tool imports the extractor's rule and "adds nothing ... changes nothing". Where the imported primitive differs from that wording (F3, F4), the spec does not say which governs.
- **S6.** Nothing in sec. 2.7 or block 41 ties the bundle's `extraction`, written by the author, to the extraction the training banks were built with. The simulated and real banks' sidecars hold w_size, the Gaussian width, n_e and the rate. A bundle declaring another `w_size_s` or `gaussian_window_s` cuts windows of another scale with no error.
