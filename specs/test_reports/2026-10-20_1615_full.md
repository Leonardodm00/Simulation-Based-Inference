PASS WITH ISSUES. Every existing smoke test passes: blocks 1, 1b, 1c, 2, 3, 7, 7b, 8, report, reset, blocks 4-6, and the Stage 1-4 suites the spec cites. Each count matches the block's Status line. Two validation targets were reproduced exactly: the D-084 measurement (L at K=99 2.9094, at K=1 3.1878) and the Stage 3 `L_primary` 1.658521682024002. The real entry point works: `run_pipeline.sh --project giulia --dry-run` and `--status` finish with exit 0, and two dry runs give identical output. 245 of 251 spec-derived tests pass. Six fail, giving one MAJOR finding, two MINOR and four NOTEs. The MAJOR one (F1) is in the block-5 streaming loader. The spec says every row is drawn exactly once per pass "whatever the shards' sizes". That holds for a small buffer: at K = 2 of 8 shards each shard's share is within 1 %. It breaks as the buffer approaches the number of shards. At K = 7 of 8, small shards get up to 27 % more draws and large shards up to 10 % fewer. For Giulia (K = 4, about 155 shards) the bias is likely small, but this was not measured. No wrong number was found on the sequencer's main path.
