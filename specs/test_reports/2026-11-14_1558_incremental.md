Block 11 amendment (also blocks 12, 16, 5's `write_shard`):
- O11.1 `BOX_DELTA == 2^-24`; both 2^-24 and 1 - 2^-24 are exactly
  representable in float32, and 1 - 2^-24 is float32's predecessor of 1.
- O11.2 Known answer for `clip_open_box(phi)`: it equals
  min(max(phi, 2^-24), 1 - 2^-24) computed by hand. It is a new float64
  array of phi's shape (0-d, empty and list inputs included) and shares no
  memory with phi; phi is unchanged. Values already in
  [2^-24, 1 - 2^-24] come back unchanged at the bit level. NaN, +inf and
  -inf each raise `FloatingPointError`.
- O11.3 Invariant: float32 of the result is strictly between 0 and 1 for
  every phi in [0, 1]. Checked on the spec's ten values, on 8,192 float64
  neighbours of each end, and on 1e5 uniform draws.
- O11.4 Reference, at fixed seeds: `sample_prior`,
  `sample_prior_given_classes` and `sample_box_prior` against the base
  commit's functions (git archive). They must agree bit for bit where the
  old value is in [2^-24, 1 - 2^-24], and elsewhere equal the clipped old
  value. The edge band is forced with class centres 1e-6 and 1 - 1e-6 at
  tau 1e-6. Classes are unchanged, and box classes are all -1 (int64).
- O11.5 Reference: a generator returning chosen values (1 - 1e-9 among
  them) leaves every stored float32 theta < 1.
- O11.6 The builder refuses a stored coordinate <= 0 or >= 1 (a
  patched-in sampler draw of 1 - 1e-9): non-zero exit, the row and axis
  named, no shard written.
- O11.7 The record tool refuses a stored theta <= 0, >= 1 or not finite,
  on arm S and arm R. It prints `REFUSE: shard <k>: <n> row(s) with a
  theta outside the open unit box (first: row <i>, axis <j>, value <v>)`
  with n, i and j as planted, exits 4 and writes no record and no
  `.sha256`. The closest float32 values to the ends (2^-24, 1 - 2^-24 and
  the smallest subnormal) are accepted. The clean banks are recorded with
  their pass line.
- O11.8 `write_shard` with another job creating the folder between its

- O11.9 Invariant: a mixture bank, a box bank and a regular-centre cohort
